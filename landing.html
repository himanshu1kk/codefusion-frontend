Capabilities

Built around how you
actually improve

Every feature is tied to a real question competitive programmers ask about their own progress.

📈
Rating History
See every contest that touched your rating — delta, rank, and a chronological chart that shows the full arc of your progress, not just the current number.
Analytics
🎯
Weakness Identification
Aggregates your submission history by problem tag and difficulty. Shows exactly which topics — dp, graphs, math — have the lowest acceptance rate in your history.
Insights
📊
Submission Analytics
Breaks down your submission log by verdict, language, and time — so you can see how often you get WA vs TLE, and how your solve speed trends across difficulty tiers.
Submissions
🔍
Problem Explorer
Filter the full Codeforces problem set by rating range and tag, and see at a glance which problems you've already solved — making targeted practice faster to set up.
Practice
📝
Problem Notes
Attach private notes to any problem — approach ideas, what tripped you up, or a reference solution sketch. Stored per-user, accessible from any device after login.
Notes
🔗
Secure Handle Verification
Links your Codeforces account via OTP — a temporary code you submit on the platform proves ownership. No password ever leaves your device. Your data is fetched via the public API under your identity.
Verification
Getting started

Four steps to full access

Account creation, email verification, handle linking, then everything unlocks. Each step is guarded — you can't skip ahead.

01
Register
Create an account with your email. A 6-digit OTP is sent immediately.
Public
02
Verify Email
Enter the OTP from your inbox to activate your account. Expires in 10 minutes.
